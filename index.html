How I work

Four things
I don't compromise on.

Eighteen years across front-end, IC design, team lead, design director and co-founder. These are the constants.

01 Any altitude
Any altitude.

Exec boards on Monday, pixels at 2am on Thursday. I can walk into any room, at any level, and add value immediately. That range is the job.

02 Design is a business lever
Design is a business lever.

Not a polish layer. Every project here is measured in revenue, retention or behaviour change. If it can't be measured, we haven't finished defining it.

03 Systems, interfaces, behaviour
Systems, interfaces, behaviour.

A business leader who happens to think in these three. The MBA and the design degree were never in tension; they're the same discipline at different zoom levels.

04 Taste is mine, throughput multiplied
Taste is mine. Throughput, multiplied.

AI runs through my entire workflow. Research, synthesis, prototyping, spec. The judgement stays human. The output is several times what one designer used to ship.

About

I started building websites at 15. Got a BSc in Product Design from Brunel, then an MBA with a focus on Digital Banking from Cranfield. Somewhere in between, I stopped being a designer who understood business and became a business leader who happens to think in systems, interfaces, and user behaviour.

I've written production code. I've run design teams of 25+. I've presented to exec boards and I've pushed pixels at 2am. Front-end engineer, IC designer, team lead, design director, co-founder. It's the reason I can walk into any room, at any altitude, and add value immediately.

AI is embedded across my entire workflow. The taste is mine. The throughput is multiplied.
